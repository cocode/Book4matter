One morning, before the others woke, Nim climbed out of the Hollow and went to find her fire.

She walked north because the wind came from the north, and she thought her fire might be hiding somewhere upwind. She crossed the wide river on the backs of the sleeping stones. She passed under trees so old they had forgotten their own names.

By the reeds at the edge of the marsh she met a heron, standing on one leg and thinking.

"I am looking for my fire," Nim told him. "Have you seen it?"

The heron considered her for a long while. "I have never seen a fire that was lost," he said at last. "Only fires that were not yet lit. Go up. Cold things remember warmth best." And he tucked his head beneath his wing and would say no more.
