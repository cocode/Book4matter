Nim was the smallest dragon in the Hollow, and the only one who had never breathed fire.

Every dusk the others gathered on the warm stones by the river and lit the dark with their breath. Great gouts of orange rolled up into the pines, and the moths came to dance in the light of it. Nim would open her mouth with the rest of them, and out would come a thin grey ribbon of smoke, and nothing more.

"It will come," said her mother, who was patient and very large. "Fire finds its own season."

But the seasons turned, and turned again, and still Nim's fire did not come.
