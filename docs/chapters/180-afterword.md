# Afterword {.section}

Every tool in this book is one its author reaches for himself. Book4matter began
as a handful of shell scripts for formatting the books he self-publishes, and it
has stayed deliberately narrow ever since: it does the typesetting a
self-publisher needs to get a clean interior onto KDP, and stops there.

It stands on the shoulders of two remarkable projects --- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org),
which reads more document formats than anyone reasonably should, and
[Typst](https://typst.app), which makes fine typesetting programmable without a
LaTeX-shaped learning cliff. Book4matter is mostly a thin, opinionated layer that
aims them at the narrow job of building a book.

This very page is the proof of the feature it demonstrates: it is a `{.section}`
heading, so it sits at the top level of the Contents --- beside the parts, not
beneath one --- and is set like a chapter rather than a part divider.

If the tool has saved you an afternoon of wrestling with margins, it has earned
its keep.
