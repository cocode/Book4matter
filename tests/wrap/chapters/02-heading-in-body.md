# Heading inside wrap body

![](../media/box.svg){.wrap-right width="2in"}

### Leader

Leader continues the turn, ending facing center.

### Follower

Follower does a 3/8 turn. It's ok to under-turn slightly, as the turn can be completed on step 3, which has no turn. Follower steps toe.

The foot is placed in line with where the leader's foot was at start. In other words, the follower is trading sides with the leader.

Leader closes their left foot to their right foot.
