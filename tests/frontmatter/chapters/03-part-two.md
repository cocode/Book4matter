# Basics

Every dance has a grammar, and this part names its pieces. These two
paragraphs were written directly beneath the part heading, in the part's own
chapter file, with no heading in between.

That makes them divider text: they must print on the same page as the
"PART 2 / BASICS" title — set below it, inset from the margins — rather than
spilling onto a page of their own. Part One's divider, which has no such
text, keeps its title at one-third height with nothing beneath.

## Terminology

Part Two exists so the contents page shows two part groups separated by
space, and so the spelled-out ordinal advances: this part's entry should
read "PART TWO · BASICS".

## Conventions

Chapter numbering runs straight through part boundaries: this is Chapter 4,
not Chapter 2, because chapters do not reset when a new part begins.
