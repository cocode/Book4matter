The part heading above carries the `unnumbered` class, so its divider page
shows no "PART N" label and its contents entry shows no "PART · " prefix —
just "APPENDIX" in the part style. It must not advance the part count
either; if a numbered part ever followed, it would still be Part Three.

the appendix entry in the contents like any other chapter.
